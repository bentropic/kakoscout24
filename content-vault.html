10 ready TikTok carousel ideas. Click "Copy image" under each photo to copy it straight to your clipboard, then paste into ChatGPT — no downloading needed. Copy the prompt below it and send.

How the image copy works: "Copy image" fetches the photo and copies it to your system clipboard as a PNG. In ChatGPT: click "Copy image" under photo 1, click into the message box, press Cmd/Ctrl+V — the photo appears attached. Then click "Copy image" under photo 2, paste again into the same message, and so on for every photo in the set, in order. Browsers only support copying one image at a time to the clipboard, so there's no single "copy all" button — this one-by-one paste is the reliable way.

If a copy button shows an error: right-click the photo → "Copy Image" works as a manual fallback in every browser.
